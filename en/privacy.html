Privacy Policy

Survival Guide (web version and Android app) · Last updated: 22 November 2026
In short: This app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. The app works without an internet connection.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not send information about your use to us or to any third party. It contains no user accounts, ads, analytics, cookies or tracking code.

2. Information that stays on your device

Some tools keep what you enter only on your own phone, in the app's local storage:

  • The emergency family card
  • Supply and medicine trackers and the log
  • Your last known position (when you use the compass and coordinates tool)
  • Settings such as the phrasebook language

This information never reaches us. It is deleted completely when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Permissions

  • Location: requested only when you open the compass and coordinates tool, to show your coordinates on screen. Your location is never sent off the device. If you refuse, the rest of the app still works.

The app does not need an internet connection and does not use the internet to transfer personal data.

4. Feedback and error reports

Tapping "Report a mistake" opens the email app on your phone with a ready-made message to dr.ozayapps@gmail.com. Sending it is entirely your choice. If you send it, we receive only what you wrote and your email address. We use this only to review the report and reply if needed, and we never share it with third parties.

5. Children

The app has a Kids' Corner. It collects no personal data from children or any other user, shows no ads and contains no outbound links for children.

6. Health information

The first-aid and health content is general educational information. The app is not a medical device, does not diagnose and does not recommend treatment. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Türkiye and the EU).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with a new date.

8. Contact
